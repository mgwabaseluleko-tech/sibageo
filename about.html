About Us

Your trusted
land surveying experts.

Established in 2020, Siba Geomatics Practitioners provides comprehensive surveying solutions across industries, with a reputation built on precision, reliability and customer satisfaction.

Vision

Beyond boundaries.

To be the vanguard of land surveying excellence in South Africa — redefining the industry through technological innovation, environmental stewardship and client collaboration, where accurate spatial data becomes the cornerstone of sustainable development.

Mission

One measurement at a time.

To be the foremost authority in land surveying, providing unparalleled precision in cadastral, topographic and construction surveys — the foundation for informed decision-making on every project we touch.

Key Factors

Innovation & creativity in the field.

Innovation

We integrate drones, GPS and LiDAR into our survey process, continuously exploring new methodologies that improve accuracy while saving time and resources for our clients.

Creativity

Imaginative problem-solving and design thinking are applied to every project, uncovering solutions to site-specific challenges beyond conventional surveying methodology.
