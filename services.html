Services

Surveying solutions,
scoped to the site.

Five core disciplines cover the full life of a property — from establishing a boundary to staking out the structures built inside it.

  • Boundary Surveys

    Accurate determination of property boundaries, resolution of boundary disputes, and detailed boundary survey reports suitable for legal and transactional purposes.

  • Topographic Surveys

    Detailed capture of natural and man-made land features, supporting land development, civil engineering and construction planning from the ground up.

  • Construction Staking

    Precise on-site placement of structures and infrastructure during construction, ensuring compliance with design plans and minimising costly errors.

  • Subdivision Planning

    Expert guidance in subdividing large tracts of land, navigating the technical and regulatory process of creating new lots or parcels.

  • GIS Mapping

    Survey data integrated into Geographic Information Systems, enabling informed decisions on land use, resource management and infrastructure planning.
